### The Story Behind My Work

Most modern teams don't fail from a lack of tools—they stall because their **AI models, workflows, and revenue data don't talk to each other**.

I started by building **AI agents, RAG architectures, and Python/FastAPI automation backends** designed to reason over messy real-world context. As I moved those systems into production, I realized the highest-leverage place to apply agentic AI and workflow orchestration is **B2B Lead Generation & Growth Operations**—turning raw web signals into clean, verified, high-converting pipelines without manual busywork.

Today, my work bridges both worlds:
1. **AI & Agentic Engineering First:** Designing context-engineered LLM systems (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude), MCP integrations, RAG pipelines, and resilient `n8n` / Python / TypeScript workflows.
2. **Aligned to B2B Lead Generation at Scale:** Plugging those AI and automation engines directly into data extraction, enrichment, email verification, and CRM orchestration—so growth teams get clean, segmented, ready-to-activate leads automatically.
